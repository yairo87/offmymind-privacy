Privacy Policy

OffMyMind · Last updated 17 September 2026

OffMyMind keeps everything you enter on your own phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for your data to sit on. We cannot see what you track, and we have no way to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is written to a database inside the app's private storage on your device:

  • the names you give your subjects — a car, a home, a licence
  • the trackers under them, with their renewal dates, cycles and reminder lead times
  • the country you attach to a tracker
  • anything you record in a tracker's history, including notes and costs
  • your settings: language, default country, reminder time and repeat

Android keeps this storage private to the app. It is removed when you delete the app.

What leaves your device

Three things, and nothing else.

The typeface. The app loads the IBM Plex typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) when it opens. Like any request to a website, this tells Google the IP address of your device and the standard headers Android sends with it. It carries none of your data — no names, dates, trackers or settings — and Google receives nothing that identifies you as a user of this app in particular. The files are cached after the first load. Google describes how it handles these requests in its Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Backups you export yourself. When you tap “Export a backup”, the app writes a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice — your own drive, your own mail, another phone. It never passes through us. Once you have sent it somewhere, that file is covered by whatever service you sent it to, not by this policy. Restoring reads a file you pick, and nothing else.

The full version. The free version holds one subject. Unlocking more is a single purchase made through Google Play, which handles the payment entirely — the app never sees your card or payment details. To know whether the full version is unlocked, the app asks Google Play whether the Google account on this phone has bought it — each time it opens until it has, and whenever you tap buy or restore — and keeps only the answer, on the device. Google shares with us the order records it provides to every developer for payments and refunds. Google describes its own handling of purchases in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your device by Android itself and delivered by your phone, not sent from anywhere. A reminder shows the tracker's name, the subject it belongs to and its date, so like any notification it may be visible on your lock screen. Your phone's notification settings control that.

Permissions the app asks for

NotificationsTo deliver the reminders you set. Asked for on first launch; refusing it leaves the rest of the app working.
Run at startupSo reminders scheduled before a restart survive it. Without this, every pending reminder is lost when the phone reboots.
InternetUsed to load the typeface and to reach Google Play for the purchase, both described above.
Google Play billingLets the full version be bought and restored through Google Play.

The app asks for no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or accounts.
